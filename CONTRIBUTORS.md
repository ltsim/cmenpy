# Contributors

This document recognizes the individuals who contribute their knowledge, time, and effort to the development and maintenance of this software. 

This project is built upon the foundational principles of Free Software and Open Science. We believe that scientific software should function as a shared resource (*commons*) for the global community. To reflect this, our development model values collective stewardship and horizontal collaboration, ensuring that knowledge remains accessible and unrestricted by institutional elitism, proprietary interests, or centralized gatekeeping.

We actively encourage and welcome contributions from researchers, developers, students, and practitioners of all backgrounds. Every contribution—whether theoretical modeling, laboratory experimentation, code optimization, bug reporting, or documentation—is considered fundamentally equal in value to the sustainability of this tool.

## How to Collaborate

We invite the scientific and open-source community to join this collective effort. You can participate by:

1. **Improving Code or Documentation:** Opening issues or submitting pull requests directly to the repository.
2. **Experimental Validation:** Sharing laboratory data or real-world use cases to improve the accuracy of the models.
3. **Peer Review:** Testing the software in different environments and providing feedback on usability and theoretical rigor.

## Institutional and Community Support

The sustainability, computational infrastructure, and core research space for this decentralized initiative are made possible through the convergence of public education, student-driven collaboration, and state support:

* **Universidad de Guadalajara & CUCEI:** This project belongs fundamentally to the public university ecosystem. We place special emphasis on the student community and researchers of the *Centro Universitario de Ciencias Exactas e Ingenierías* (CUCEI), whose collective talent, horizontal cooperation, and dedication drive the development of this tool as a shared resource for society.
* **LTSIM:** Acting as part of the internal infrastructure of CUCEI, provides the physical and computational space where this open-source research is collectively developed and maintained.
* **SECIHTI:** We acknowledge the public funding and institutional support provided by the *Secretaría de Ciencia, Innovación, Tecnología e Humanidades* (SECIHTI), which contributes to the advancement of open science, humanistic technological development, and the democratization of knowledge.
